About

I am a Mechanical Engineer at Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution (WHOI). My work focuses on the design, simulation, analysis, and fabrication of ocean engineering systems that support marine research, ocean observation, and field operations. My areas of expertise include hydrodynamics, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), hydro-mechanical systems, mooring technologies, AUV dynamics, and the design of reliable oceanographic systems for deployment in challenging marine environments.

I hold an MS in Mechanical Engineering from UMass Dartmouth, where my thesis focused on VIV suppression in marine stretch hose moorings using Large Eddy Simulation (LES), motivated by passive acoustic monitoring for North Atlantic Right Whales.
